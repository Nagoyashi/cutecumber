# v0.11.0 — Sites, not just pages

Phase B of the creator-platform overhaul. A cutecumber page grows into a small
**multi-page site**: keep your home at `cutecumber.cc/you`, and add subpages like
`/you/about` or `/you/shop` — each a full builder page, linked by a tiny site nav.
Still zero-JS, zero-tracker, in-contract on every page a visitor loads.

## For visitors

- **Subpages** at `/<username>/<slug>` render server-side with the same promises
  as the home page — zero JavaScript, no cookies, WCAG-AA.
- A **site nav** links a creator's pages (home + published subpages). It's
  theme-aware (inherits the page's colors), zero-JS, and hidden when there's only
  one page.

## For creators

- The builder gains a **page switcher**: 🏠 home + your subpages + "＋ page". Add,
  rename, delete, and edit each page with the full section editor, and publish
  each independently.
- **Additive & safe:** your existing page simply *is* your home — nothing
  migrated, nothing lost.

## Under the hood

- A new `pages` table holds subpages (home stays in the user row), all queries
  user-scoped (IDOR), capped at 8 subpages, with the slug immutable after create
  (the URL is the product). Orphan-image GC and account deletion both cover
  subpage photos. (`DECISIONS.md` #41)
