# v0.11.3 — 36 new avatars + a fully-live theme preview

## 36 new avatars

The character set grows from 12 to **48** — 36 new friends to pick from (peachy,
ducky, tako, bamboo, dino, boba, hammy, jelly, and more). Choose any in your
profile; they're curated, self-contained SVGs, same as the originals.

## Everything previews live now

Tweaking your theme updates the preview **as you go** — and not just colours:
**decorations, heading font, button shape/style, background pattern and page
layout** all reflect immediately. Nothing saves until you hit save.

- Colours and text keep updating instantly in-place; structural tweaks re-render
  the preview from your unsaved settings via a new dashboard-only preview route
  (`/dash/theme-preview`). **Public pages stay zero-JS** — the preview is your own
  real page, rendered server-side.

## Decorations easier to see

The decoration picker tiles are larger — and because decorations now preview live,
you see the real tiled effect on your page the moment you toggle one.
